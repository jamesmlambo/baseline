7. WhatsApp-assisted booking and branded live price list —
CONFIRMED
1.   Customer requests accommodation via WhatsApp and provides intended dates.
2.   Reception sends a colourful branded secure price-list link populated from current date-specific
     availability.
3.   Customer selects room type/room, adds breakfast/dinner or other extras, enters name, surname
     and phone number, and sees an itemised total.
4.   System records the request; a time-limited inventory hold may apply under approved policy.
5.   Issue unpaid invoice or payment request containing banking details and optional secure
     payment link.
6.   Customer pays by manual EFT or payment gateway; staff verify manual EFT against bank
     settlement.
7.   On confirmed payment, update invoice to paid, issue receipt and confirm reservation; refresh
     shared inventory.
8.   Send document links by WhatsApp and PDF attachments by email; log communications and
     delivery status.

Do not guarantee availability merely because a price-list link was opened; revalidate inventory at
selection and confirmation. Decide how long temporary holds last and what happens if payment
arrives late.

8. Secure guest portal: room QR + SMS links — CONFIRMED
The room-door QR code identifies a physical room, not the identity of its occupant. The client has
explicitly decided to KEEP both room QR codes and secure SMS portal links. Both routes use the
same guest services backend.

8.1 Mandatory QR verification
9.   Guest scans the room QR code; only a generic verification screen appears.
10. System identifies the active checked-in stay for that room without displaying guest identity.
11. Send a six-digit one-time code to the phone number registered on the booking.
12. Guest enters OTP; apply five-minute expiry, rate limits, attempt limits and anti-enumeration
     safeguards.
13. After successful verification, establish a per-stay secure session; allow access only to authorised
     stay-specific services.
14. Expire/revoke access at checkout, room reassignment or security incident; do not expose
     previous guests’ messages to future occupants.

If the registered phone is unavailable, reception must verify identity through a documented
recovery process. Approved: a verified booker can authorise additional guest phone numbers
through a logged, secure process; staff-assisted recovery requires identity verification. Staff should
never see OTP values.

9. Checkout, payments and room turnover — CONFIRMED +
RECOMMENDED controls
15. Guest initiates checkout through verified QR/SMS portal or reception.
16. System checks folio, unpaid extras, unverified EFT, disputes and required staff approvals.
17. If the balance is unresolved, show payment/reception options; do not silently complete
    checkout.
18. When checkout is approved, record departure, close the stay and revoke room-service access.
19. Trigger thank-you/safe-travels message and create a housekeeping turnover task.
20. Room moves to Dirty; assigned worker cleans; inspector verifies; room becomes Ready for the
    next arrival.

A zero balance is a recommended default gate, with controlled manager overrides for legitimate
credit/corporate arrangements. The specific accounting rules need owner approval.

   Workers self-complete profiles including name, contact details and selfie/photo; administrator

   Reception allocates housekeeping jobs; authorised owner/supervisor allocates gardening and

   Workers see only their own tasks, shift schedule, notifications and attendance history.

   Support leave, public holidays, authorised exceptions and coverage changes; avoid non-urgent

11. Housekeeping and maintenance — CONFIRMED
Routine cleaning/refresh: sweep, mop, scrub shower, make bed, replenish sugar/coffee, verify towels
and general room readiness. Differentiate full cleaning from light refresh.

Maintenance/gardening task catalogue: pressure-wash pavement; maintain plants/flowers; clean
pool; clean VIP 1 and VIP 2; scrub fish pond; trim/cut grass; polish floors; painting; clean braai
stands; general repairs.

   Every job has location, assignee, checklist, priority, due date, completion evidence and status.
   Track maintenance history by room/asset, preventive schedules, equipment warranties and
    supplier costs.
   Stock ledger for towels, toiletries, coffee, sugar, cleaning supplies and other consumables; low-

12. Monday deep clean and room inspection — CONFIRMED
   Monday checklist: deep clean under beds; replace/refresh drinking water; clean aluminium
    doors and windows; microwave, kettle, mirrors, floors, shower and beds.
   Inspector receives assigned rooms, checks each criterion and scores cleanliness/condition using
    an agreed scale.
   Inspection produces a report and sends a result notification to the relevant worker.
   Unsatisfactory items create rework tasks with details, due time and re-inspection; satisfactory
    rooms can be approved ready.
   Keep inspection history and optional photos; do not conflate cleaning completion with
    inspection approval.

25. Version 5.2 — approved implementation clarifications
   Exact room commitment: the booking engine must reserve a specific physical room at booking
    time; enforce transactional uniqueness across date ranges. For external channels that transmit
    category-level bookings, map the confirmed reservation immediately to an available physical
    room or flag an exception for staff before promising the assignment. Reassignments require
    reason, authorisation and audit trail.
   Extensive communications: automate booking enquiries, price-list follow-up, unpaid invoices,
    payment confirmation, arrival information, service alerts, departure-day reminders, farewell
    messages, feedback requests, and owner escalation where appropriate. Distinguish operational
    from marketing messages; support templates, language, guest preferences, consent, delivery
    status, throttling, retry limits and provider costs.
   QR and SMS access: both channels lead to the same stay-bound guest portal. Room QR requires
    OTP to the booking phone. Verified booker can add authorised guest numbers; authorisations
    are logged and revocable. A phone number alone is not proof of room entitlement. Staff-assisted
    recovery must verify identity.
   Attendance rules: administrators configure valid clock-in and clock-out windows per shift.
    Compare device events against assigned roster, leave and rest days. Show worked, absent, late,
    incomplete, off-duty and pending-review statuses; calculate days worked for exports, with
    authorised manual corrections and audit trail.
   Financial controls: Instant EFT appears first but manual EFT and card payments remain
    available. Do not treat proof of payment as bank settlement. Dual-approve refunds, retain
    immutable document records and produce reconciliation exports.

   No offline booking commitments: securely cached arrival and room information is read-only
    with access controls, encryption, expiry and sync indicators; staff must not confirm new
    reservations without online availability verification.

26. Confirmed attendance hardware — integration requirements
Installed device: Hikvision DS-K1T342MFWX-E1 Face Recognition Terminal. This is existing
equipment and must be retained. Source: manufacturer datasheet, DS-K1T342MFWX-E1, V1.0 (3
August 2023), supplied by client.

   Authentication supported by datasheet: face, fingerprint, M1 card and personal PIN. Capacity:
    1,500 faces, 3,000 fingerprints, 3,000 cards and 150,000 stored events.
   Connectivity and integration indicated by datasheet: Ethernet 10/100, Wi-Fi, ISAPI and ISUP 5.0.
    Supports time synchronisation. These advertised interfaces do not, by themselves, prove a
    working attendance API on the deployed firmware.
   Phase 0 proof of concept: obtain installed firmware/version and network details; verify
    authorised device access; test reading a sample punch with timestamp and employee ID; test
    repeated polling/event duplication, outages and recovery; document any gateway or on-premise
    connector required.
   Keep biometric templates on the terminal/approved biometric management system rather than
    copying them into the guesthouse web database. Import only necessary attendance events and
    employee mapping, with POPIA/privacy review and appropriate access control.
   PIN/card fallback must remain available as an approved attendance method. Match all device
    identities to employee profiles and shifts; preserve raw punches and derived attendance
    calculations separately.
   Acceptance test: a test employee punches in and out on the existing device; the correct shift and
    date appear in employee and manager dashboards, including late/incomplete/off-day edge cases,
    and weekly/monthly payroll-ready exports.

Phase 0 must compare (A) custom Laravel booking/operations with a commercially supported
channel manager and (B) a hybrid PMS/channel manager with custom Laravel modules. A fully
bespoke OTA connector is not presumed feasible. NightsBridge, Rentals United and NextPax are
candidate providers only; agencies must substantiate current Booking.com and LekkeSlaap support,
commercial terms, rate/availability update behaviour, sandbox access, webhooks, and contract
exit/data export.

Determine which system owns authoritative availability, how conflicts and delayed/pull-based
updates are handled, and how each external reservation maps to a named physical room. Provide
scheduled reconciliation, alerting, stale-sync warnings, retries, and an overbooking exception queue.
Do not claim guaranteed real-time synchronisation without provider evidence.

Required vertical-slice demonstration: OTA/direct booking → inventory/room assignment → payment
status → invoice/receipt → check-in/check-out → housekeeping task, with retries and exception
scenarios.

31. Booking and physical-room data model — IMPLEMENTATION
RULE
   Sell and price by accommodation category and date, but persist each booking’s named physical-
    room assignment as a separate audited record made at booking time.
   Enforce room/date exclusion transactionally; use category-night inventory calculations,
    maintenance blocks and concurrency tests.
   For OTA category-only bookings, automatically allocate an eligible room immediately when
    ingested; if none can be safely allocated, quarantine and alert staff rather than silently
    overbook.
   Room reassignment is authorised, audited, and updates guest portal entitlements and
    housekeeping assignments; distinguish booking status, payment status and room-readiness
    status.
   Reservations arriving after a hold has expired must re-check inventory before confirmation; late
    EFTs require a documented rebooking/refund path.

32. Hikvision attendance integration and workforce privacy
Confirmed model spelling: Hikvision DS-K1T342MFWX-E1. Manufacturer datasheet states 150,000
events; a reviewer found a conflicting reseller claim of 100,000. Use the manufacturer datasheet as
the provisional specification and confirm on installed firmware/device during Phase 0.

Use wired Ethernet where practical, time synchronisation, and UPS protection for
device/router/reception equipment. Test ISAPI/ISUP event retrieval, event pagination, missing
records, network outages, restart recovery, time-zone handling and employee mapping.

Keep raw device punches distinct from derived check-in/out, lateness and absence decisions. Import
idempotently using a robust composite identity and deduplication strategy (not sequence numbers
alone); retain source event identifiers where available. Biometrics stay on the authorised
device/controller, not the Laravel database.

Offer PIN/M1 card as a genuine non-punitive attendance alternative. Publish an employee privacy
notice, access controls, correction procedure, lawful-basis assessment and retention schedule, to be
reviewed for POPIA compliance.

33. OTP, guest access and messaging controls
   Room-door QR codes identify rooms only; never reveal occupant names or booking details
    before verification. Use six-digit OTP, five-minute expiry, attempt/rate limits, anti-enumeration
    and anti-SMS-pumping monitoring.
   OTP goes to the original booking number or an additional number explicitly authorised by the
    verified booker; log authorisation and revocation. If SMS is unavailable, permit controlled
    reception recovery; evaluate WhatsApp/email OTP as optional alternative channels after
    identity and security review.
   Bind sessions to active stay and permitted room; revoke upon checkout, room reassignment or
    security incident. Protect historical invoice QR lookup separately behind reception
    authentication.
   Use approved WhatsApp templates/API for transactional automations, plus click-to-chat for
    informal enquiries where useful. Track delivery failures, retries, opt-outs/consent and
    templates. Phase 0 must obtain provider quotes and set per-message budget forecasts, abuse
    alerts and a monthly spend cap.
   Guest checkout must check folio and unresolved charges; use an approved manager exception
    for legitimate credit arrangements. On completion, issue farewell message and housekeeping
    turnover; do not duplicate messages on retries.

34. Payments, financial integrity and holds
   Start with one gateway where practicable to simplify webhook reconciliation; retain Instant
    EFT, ordinary EFT and card options as approved.
   Verify gateway signatures, idempotency and missing/duplicate/out-of-order webhook handling.
    Run scheduled settlement reconciliation. Proof of EFT is not verified payment.
   Separate quotation, issued invoice, receipt, credit note and payment ledger. Corrections use
    audited adjustments rather than silently overwriting issued financial documents.
   Reservation holds are STILL PENDING client approval. Reviewers propose two-hour
    online/WhatsApp holds and 12–24-hour manual EFT holds; a second reviewer recommends
    varying holds with arrival proximity. These are proposals only.
   Refunds require dual approval; record reason, approvers, gateway transaction, reversal and
    reconciliation status.

35. Guest services, housekeeping and physical operations —
ENHANCEMENTS
   Introduce a key/key-card handover and return log linked to each stay, including lost-key
    incidents and authorised access exceptions. Self-check-in and smart-lock integration remain
    optional and require separate approval.
   Consider ingesting Booking.com/LekkeSlaap guest messages into the unified inbox if provider
    APIs/contracts permit; otherwise provide staff with a clear manual handoff workflow.
   Add estimated cleaning effort by room type and task type, configurable by managers, to support
    fair staff rosters and Sunday laundry workloads.

   Keep Monday deep-clean checklist and inspection scores, rework notifications and sign-off by
    authorised inspector/owner; record before/after photos where appropriate.
   Day passes, braai, swimming pool, VIP 1/VIP 2, spa and restaurant extras need service inventory,
    booking capacities, time slots, charge-to-room and incident workflows.

36. Compliance verification register — NOT LEGAL ADVICE
Reviewer A recommends guest-register fields, a two-year retention period, VAT/tax invoice rules,
PAIA/POPIA notices, TGCSA grading controls and optional tourism levies. Reviewer B likewise cites
immigration guest-register rules and biometric protections. These are reviewer assertions to be
validated against current South African law by qualified legal/tax advisors before launch.

Implement configurable retention and restricted access, legal-hold exceptions, documented
deletion/anonymisation, and a register capture workflow after legal sign-off. Do not hardcode an
unverified two-year deletion rule or any tax thresholds into production.

Only display official tourism grading stars when documentary evidence of valid grading exists;
publish an anti-phishing policy and never request payment-card credentials over chat.
